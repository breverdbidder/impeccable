# Surface: index.html (Ecosystem Village)

Scope: the whole page, a single-file canvas simulation. Visitor mode: Experience. The artifact (the living village) leads; the interface recedes into a cabinet frame around it.

Audience and job: a founder showing partners and investors that BidDeed.AI, zonewise.ai, and winnerdataai.com are one ecosystem exchanging data. The viewer watches for one to three minutes and should be able to retell who sends what to whom.

Content and proof: three hubs, fifteen agents, visible packets, chat bubbles, a ledger of sent and received packets, deals closing when BidDeed.AI holds zoning plus metrics. All dialogue and figures are synthetic and labeled.

Constraints: 800x600 logical canvas scaled to the container; no runtime dependencies beyond web fonts; brand names spelled exactly; brief-pinned 32-bit top-down RPG village with dirt paths, water, neon signs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One village, one economy, watched through an arcade cabinet at dusk. It refuses the category default (a bright pastoral sandbox with a floating title and a sentence of instructions) and the opposite (a dashboard with a game embedded). The neon signs are the hero: the brief names them, so the day-night cycle exists to make them matter.

OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo cabinet (#0b0c1e to #15173a) around a saturated village (grass #2f6b2a family, dirt #8a6a45, water #2d6fb3). Hub neons: BidDeed.AI sky blue #38bdf8, zonewise.ai mint #34d399, winnerdataai.com violet #c084fc. JRPG status windows (navy fill, 2px light border) for every in-canvas readout and bubble. Type: Press Start 2P for display and labels, VT323 for running text and the ticker. Controls are cabinet buttons: flat pixel rectangles with a pressed state. Icons are drawn bitmaps, never glyphs. Scanlines are a toggle, off by default.

STORY: The viewer sees three named hubs glowing and small couriers carrying lit packets along the roads. Bubbles name the data. The ledger below confirms counts. When a deal closes, BidDeed.AI flares and the ticker logs it. The viewer understands the three brands as one supply chain.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Marquee title on top (one line, neon tube). Directly under it the screen: canvas at full container width (max 800), bezel around it. Inside the canvas: village fills the frame, HUD windows top-left (clock, phase of day) and top-right (packets in transit, deals closed). Below the screen the control deck: pause, speed, night, scanlines on the left; the exchange ledger (one row per hub) on the right; the ticker beneath. The primary action is watching; the one control that matters, Pause, is first.

FORM: Candidate 5 of 7 (dusk festival village on an arcade CRT), seed key 88e98e5d, roll ran degraded (no challengers). Signature interaction: the dusk transition, where ambient light falls and the three neon signs bloom while packets become lanterns. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out on window reveals, pulse on neon only, no section fades.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
